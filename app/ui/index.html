lolcommits

a selfie for every commit

lolcommits takes a photo when you commit and puts the commit message on it in big meme letters. In a year you have a timelapse of your face while shipping things.

fun, and fair

Photos of people are personal data. lolcommits keeps team spirit and the GDPR on the same side:

Stays with you

  • Every photo stays on this computer.
  • No upload, no cloud, no tracking.
  • Delete anything, any time.

If you share with your team

  • Voluntary – you pick every picture after a preview.
  • Date only, no times, no statistics per person.
  • Take it back with one click.
Using it at work?Pictures of colleagues are employee data. Before a team gallery starts, check with your data protection officer (and works council, if you have one) and agree how long team pictures are kept.

every commit, every repo

lolcommits hooks into git for all your repositories. Committing stays instant – the photo is taken in the background.

Note: git clients with their own built-in git (some GUI apps) may not trigger it. Terminal, VS Code, JetBrains and Apple git work.
